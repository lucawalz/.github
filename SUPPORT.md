# Support

- Questions and usage problems: open an issue with the bug or feature form and include the version, platform and the exact steps taken.
- Security vulnerabilities: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and report privately, never in a public issue.
- Conduct concerns: follow the reporting section of [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

These projects are maintained by one person in spare time. Responses are best effort and come through the issue tracker; there is no paid or private support channel.
